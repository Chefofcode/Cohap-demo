A 501(c)(3) Nonprofit

Breaking the Cycle of Homelessness.Restoring hope & building futures across Greater Houston.

COHAP OUTREACH CORPORATION is dedicated to preventing homelessness and rebuilding stability for those most at risk, including veterans, young adults aging out of foster care, domestic violence survivors, and individuals returning from incarceration. We provide protection, essential resources, and pathways to independence so every person has the chance to thrive.

Your support fuels hope, resilience, and second chances. With your partnership, we can ensure no one is left without shelter and every individual can step into tomorrow with confidence.

Our Programs & Services

Six structured programs designed to help individuals and families rebuild their lives with dignity.

Housing

Emergency shelter, transitional housing, and homebuyer education for long-term stability.

Learn More →

Workforce Vocational Certification & GED Program

GED prep, vocational training, financial literacy, and life-skills coaching for reintegration.

Learn More →

Entrepreneurship

Mentoring, business coaching, and seminars from successful entrepreneurs to launch your venture.

Learn More →

Mental Wellness

Stress management, mindfulness, addiction support, and holistic practices for emotional recovery.

Learn More →

Mentoring

Youth mentoring for ages 12–25: GED prep, career exploration, college guidance, and community service.

Learn More →

Veterans

Dedicated support for veterans transitioning back to civilian life: housing, employment, and mental-health resources.

Learn More →
Our Mission

To break the cycle of recidivism and homelessness through support, advocacy, and education by providing a structured plan and pathway for formerly incarcerated individuals, domestic violence survivors, veterans, and aged-out foster care young adults.
